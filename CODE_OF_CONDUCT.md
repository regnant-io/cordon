# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Reviewing work on its merits, and saying plainly what is wrong with it.
- Accepting that a well-argued objection to your patch is a service, not an
  attack.
- Assuming a contributor is acting in good faith until shown otherwise.

Behaviour that does not:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private GitHub
security advisory or by email to the address in `Cargo.toml`. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions,
or ban contributors whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
